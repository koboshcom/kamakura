Uploaded videos from authenticated Telegram, WhatsApp and Discord private chats enter one shared adaptive path. First requested window is 5 seconds and at most 2 frames, with explicit separately configured transcription only. Stop when enough. The returned opaque handle can be passed to inspect_video_url with next, start/end or timestamps, including from voice delegation in the same owner's Discord DM context. Handles never grant another owner or channel access.

Acquisition requires the full attachment body under transport streaming caps, not full decoding or full STT. WhatsApp uses Baileys image/video key derivation with encrypted SHA256, truncated HMAC and plaintext SHA256/length verification before any model input. Unknown MIME, failed integrity or resource caps produce unavailable evidence, never invented media contents. Audio and outgoing WhatsApp voice remain disabled.

Sources are copied by bounded tar upload into the authenticated owner's existing box, random generated names only. A fixed helper rejects symlinks, hardlinks, devices, oversized files or changed SHA256; it snapshots the validated file privately before decoding. No host shell, new public service, credentials or arbitrary source paths. Sources live in the box until expiry or box recreation. Handles/cache/ledgers are core-memory-only and not restored after restart; abandoned box sources older than 30 minutes are removed at the next helper invocation. Normal expiry is 5 minutes with a 30-second sweep, best-effort source deletion without creating a box solely for cleanup. No promise of retention across sandbox image rebuild.

Uploads use the same cumulative limits as URL inspection. Defaults are 20 unique seconds, 60 actual processed seconds, 8 frames, 32 MiB source/transfer bytes, 25 seconds wall time including STT, 8 attempts, 32 handles and 16 MiB normalized cache. Overlap and replay use cached results without new decoding/STT. Failed attempts close the inspection; expiry and retries of the same inbound artifact do not reset its budget. Decoder keyframe padding is charged, so a nominal 5-second request may process more than 5 seconds internally. Last-source windows may be shorter than the requested interval and output notes source duration.

Images retain bounded static JPEG/PNG/WebP normalization and vision gating. Audio/doc behavior is not broadened. Transcription URL/key/model is explicit, on core only, with zero fallback or retry. All media and transcript contents are untrusted evidence, not instructions.

Offline synthetic fixtures cover all three platform entrypoints, replay/next/timestamps, owner/channel isolation, expiry/source cleanup, WhatsApp decrypt/integrity and adapter authorization, and real local FFmpeg processing without network/provider calls. Live platform uploads and STT/vision providers remain unverified.
