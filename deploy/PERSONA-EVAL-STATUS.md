Paired persona evaluation, 7 October 2026.

Two generic prompt candidates were tested through the actual think pipeline on seven fixed four-turn exchanges each. Delivery was captured, not sent to Telegram. The cases covered plain and shorthand project updates, topic changes, memory correction, disappointment, and requested depth with acknowledgment silence. Baseline provenance uses the first six original rows plus the separately captured original taste/depth row; the contaminated appended row was replaced.

Each comparison randomized pair order and A/B assignment independently, with the condition key withheld from its independent AI reviewer until review completion. These are small qualitative AI assessments, not human feedback or statistically significant results. The second candidate was informed by the first review, so the two assessments are not independent replications.

First comparison decoded to three candidate preferences, three original preferences and one tie. The revised comparison also decoded to three candidate preferences, three original preferences and one tie. Neither demonstrated an overall improvement. The original persona is retained; no candidate persona rollout is claimed. Do not keep tuning these same seven cases until a favorable result appears.

Shared weaknesses include invented project defects, premature emotional framing, repeated praise or correction recaps, and polished joke signatures. Candidate variants improved some correction and topic-change cases but regressed others. A larger held-out evaluation and preferably real human preference feedback are needed before a confident general improvement claim.
